What HubbleNest is

HubbleNest is a Progressive Web App for groups that want to stay connected without surrendering their data. It runs entirely in your browser, installs to your home screen, and stores what you create in Google Firebase (Firebase project “hubblenest”) and Cloudinary. There is no advertising, no tracking pixels and no resale of anything.

How it is built

The entire application is hand-written HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks to audit, no build pipeline to trust blindly. Authentication, realtime data and push delivery run on Firebase; file storage runs on Cloudinary. Private messages are end-to-end encrypted in your browser before they ever reach a database. The ranking that orders your feed is documented in the app itself, in plain language.

What we will never do

We will not sell your data, inject sponsored content into your Spaces, or make a private conversation public because it is convenient. Public exposure exists only where a member deliberately turns it on — post by post, Space by Space.
