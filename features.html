Core features

Each feature below is a real, working part of HubbleNest — not a roadmap item. Open the app and use it today.

Spaces

Create private hubs for your class, team, fellowship or club. Chat, post announcements, share files and links, and manage members — with an invite code or QR you control.

Community feed

Post to the whole community with images and file attachments. A transparent “For You” ranking favours freshness and engagement — and you can always switch to Latest.

Private chat

Encrypted one-to-one messaging. Add people to your contacts by username and start chatting instantly — no request ping-pong between people who already know each other.

Direct Share

Hand files to another member with a six-letter code or a QR. Files stream to Cloudinary and appear on their screen in real time, with genuine progress and success states.

Arcade

Write HTML, CSS and JavaScript games in the built-in editor, or upload a whole project folder in any text-based language. Playable games run in a locked sandbox.

Notifications

In-app alerts for messages, requests and mentions — plus real off-app web push that reaches members even when HubbleNest is closed.

Contacts-first privacy

Your People page shows only the contacts you added. Nothing about you is public unless you deliberately publish it.

Installable PWA

HubbleNest installs to your home screen, works offline thanks to a service worker, and updates itself safely after every release.
