Community guidelines

Five principles that keep every nest worth nesting in.

Open HubbleNest free Report a problem

1. Treat people like people

No harassment, hate speech, threats or deliberate humiliation. Disagree with ideas as hard as you like; extend to persons the respect you would want in your own living room.

2. Share honestly

Label your content truthfully. Do not impersonate others, misrepresent files you share, or upload games and code that are not yours to share.

3. Keep private things private

What is said in a private chat or a private Space stays there. Do not screenshot-shame, do not republish members’ words or files elsewhere without consent.

4. Keep it safe

No malware, no phishing links, no exploits — including in uploaded game projects. Game code runs in a strict sandbox by design; attempts to escape it endanger everyone’s nest and are removed.

5. Fix it together

See something that breaks these principles? Report it through the contact page or to a Space administrator. Administrators manage membership in their Spaces; the team handles platform-level issues.
