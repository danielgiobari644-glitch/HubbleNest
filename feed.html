A feed that respects your attention

The HubbleNest feed is one shared community timeline. Posts are ranked by a transparent algorithm you can read in the app — and you can always switch to strict chronological order.

What you can do

Posting is real, rich and readable — multi-line by design, never one cramped line.

Write properly

Bold, italic, headings, lists, code and links render as real formatting. Posts keep your line breaks and paragraphs.

Attach anything

Images, video, audio and documents upload to Cloudinary and preview inline.

Like & comment

Threaded comments per post, live counters, and notifications to the author.

Understand the ranking

Freshness, engagement, your connections and what you have already seen — all explained in-app, with no hidden signals and no ads.

Public web posts

Want to share a post with the world? Mark it public and it gets its own discoverable web page at /feed/post/… — private posts never do.
