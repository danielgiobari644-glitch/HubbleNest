✦ Simple, Powerful Sovereignty

How HubbleNest works in 3 clear steps.

From creation to active collaboration in less than sixty seconds.

01

Craft Your Sanctuary

Choose your space name, category, and lifespan — whether permanent for enduring teams or auto-expiring for pop-up workshops. Customize your sanctuary with Cloudinary cover art.

02

Invite via QR or Code

Generate a printable high-contrast QR code or share your unique six-character Space Code (like HN-7K4P92). New arrivals submit requests, and administrators approve each member with one tap.

03

Communicate & Vault

Engage in fluid discussion streams, publish pinned announcements, upload PDFs, audio and video to the shared Cloudinary vault, and converse in client-side encrypted private channels.
