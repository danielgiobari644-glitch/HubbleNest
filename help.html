Getting started

Open HubbleNest, create an account with your email, pick a username, and you are in. Your username is how people add you as a contact — choose it carefully because it is exact.

Joining a Space with a code

Tap Join, enter the code you were given (it looks like HN-7K4P92), and press Join. A valid code adds you to the Space instantly — no approval step. Codes are case-insensitive and ignore extra spaces.

Adding contacts by username

Open People, type the exact username of the person you want to add, verify the profile card that appears, and press Add to contacts. Contacts can chat with you instantly without sending you a request.

Private chat and encryption

Chats with your contacts open directly. Messages are encrypted in your browser (AES-256-GCM) before they are stored, and only the two of you can decrypt them — on any device you sign in from.

Sharing files with Direct Share

Open Direct Share, choose Send, and add files of any type — code, archives, documents, media. Give the six-letter code or QR to the other member; whatever you add appears on their screen live, with download buttons.

Posting and formatting

The feed composer supports real formatting: bold, italic, underline, headings, lists, inline code and links — use the toolbar buttons above the composer. Your line breaks are always preserved.

Creating and uploading games

In the Arcade, write HTML/CSS/JS in the editor with live preview, or upload a project folder in any text-based language. Projects with an HTML entry are playable in a sandbox; other-language projects are stored and shared as readable source.

Notifications and push

In-app alerts always work. For notifications when HubbleNest is closed, enable push when prompted; the project owner configures the Web Push certificate once, and it persists across app updates.

Troubleshooting

If something fails with a permissions message, open Get Help → Connection Doctor: it diagnoses your session live and points to the exact fix. To refresh a stale install, use Repair app cache inside the doctor.

Account and data

You can change your display name, photo and bio in Profile. Signing out removes this device’s push token. Deleting your account data is available on request through the contact page.

Still stuck? Contact the team — real people answer.
