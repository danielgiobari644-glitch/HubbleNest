Privacy policy

Plain-language commitments backed by technical architecture. Last updated: October 2026.

Open HubbleNest free Read the terms

What we collect

An account consists of an email address, a display name, a username, an optional profile photo and biography, and the content you create (posts, messages, files, games). Technical data limited to your FCM push token (if you enable notifications) and standard Firebase authentication identifiers is also stored. We collect nothing else — no analytics profiles, no advertising identifiers, no location.

Where your data lives

Account and content data is stored in Google Firestore for the Firebase project “hubblenest”. Files and media are stored on Cloudinary. Private message content is end-to-end encrypted in your browser before storage: plaintext never reaches the database. Passwords are handled exclusively by Firebase Authentication and never visible to the application.

Who can see what

Private chat is readable only by its two participants (enforced by encryption plus Firestore security rules). Spaces are visible only to their members. Your profile card (name, username, photo, bio) is visible to signed-in members. Nothing is published to the open web unless you explicitly mark it public — post by post, Space by Space, game by game.

Cookies and storage

HubbleNest uses no tracking cookies. Browser local storage holds only UI preferences and local caches; IndexedDB holds an offline cache of Firestore data. You can clear both at any time from the Connection Doctor’s “Repair app cache”.

Your rights

You can correct your profile in the app at any time, sign out (which removes this device’s push token), and request export or deletion of your account data through the contact page. Deletion requests are executed against the Firestore and Cloudinary project records.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced in the app’s announcement channel before they take effect. The version above is always current.
